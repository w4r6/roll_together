Roll Together only sends the data needed to synchronize videos between users watching together.



What data is transmitted:

1\. Video playback state (playing/paused)

2\. Current video timestamp (in seconds)

3\. Room IDs for synchronization



Key points:

1\. Data transmission is temporary and session-based

2\. No permanent storage of user data

3\. No third-party data sharing

4\. Users can disconnect anytime

5\. Local preferences (colors) stay in browser only
